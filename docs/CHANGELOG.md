Fixed by giving the `Sidebar` in `layout.tsx` `className="sticky top-0 h-svh ..."` — `h-svh` sizes directly off the viewport instead of depending on an ancestor's resolved height, and `sticky top-0` keeps it pinned full-height while the main content scrolls on pages taller than the viewport.
